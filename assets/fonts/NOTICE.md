# Fonts

Figtree, Fraunces, and IBM Plex Mono are bundled as local assets (no
runtime network fetch, consistent with AutoCarnet's offline-first
principle) and are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which
permits embedding in applications, including commercial ones.

- Figtree — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Figtree
- Fraunces — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces
- IBM Plex Mono — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono
